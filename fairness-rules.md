# Fairness rules
- Aspirants listed alphabetically or randomly rotated, never by payment or popularity
- Identical profile template and equal fields for everyone
- Flat equal fee, or sponsorship by a neutral party
- Aspirants edit their own profiles; changes pass moderator review
- Published content policy: no hate speech, incitement or disinformation (NCIC), with takedown and appeals process
- Published terms and an independent advisory panel (clergy, elders, youth, civil society)
