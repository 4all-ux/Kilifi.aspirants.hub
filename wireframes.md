# Wireframe outline
- **Home:** hero search ("Find your ward"), county map, seat shortcuts, countdown, latest events
- **Constituency page:** ward list, then seat tabs (MP, Woman Rep) with aspirant cards
- **Ward page:** MCA aspirants, ward events, voter Q&A for that ward
- **Aspirant page:** header (photo, name, party, seat), pledges, bio, manifesto, events, Q&A, share button
- **Compare page:** pick seat, pick up to 3 aspirants, side-by-side pledges and background
